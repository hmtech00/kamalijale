Fashion Creative · Stylist · Art Director — "The Royal Stylist"

Kamal Ijale

Full name Kamal Ijale Oredein · based between Milan and Lagos · pronouns he/him.

A graduate of Babcock University, Kamal's path into fashion began close to home: his mother, Her Royal Majesty Olotu-Erelu Aderonke Agbeke Ijale, ran a fabric merchandising business that gave him an early, hands-on education in textiles. That foundation carried him from Lagos Island's fabric markets into the orbit of Lagos Fashion Week, and eventually into styling for magazines and private clients.

In 2018 he launched two ventures side by side: Life of Kamal, his personal styling and image-consultation practice, and All Materialized by Kamal Ijale, a fabric production and merchandising business based on Lagos Island. With over seven years in the industry as of 2024, he has said he'd rather see the fashion world built on collaboration than competition — and he's working toward founding his own fashion house.

His editorial work has been published in L'Officiel and featured by Vogue Italia. In February 2026, the Afro Fashion Association selected him as one of five creatives — stylist and creative lead — for Communities at Work, a talent program born from AFA's collaboration with Vogue Italia and Camera Moda, staged during Milan Fashion Week. On the client side, his styling has shaped seven Ola Reay collections — spanning menswear and womenswear across Spring/Summer, Fall/Winter and Resort seasons — alongside private styling for public figures including a celebrity gospel artist and a corporate managing director.

"Styling empowers individuals to express their unique personality, boost confidence, and make a statement through their fashion choices."
