Fashion Creative · Stylist · Art Director

Kamal Ijale

Based between Milan and Lagos — pronouns he/him.

Kamal works across three connected disciplines: personal styling and image consultation for individual clients, fabric and editorial styling for magazines and campaigns, and full creative direction for fashion brands and collections. The throughline is the same in every project — building a visual identity strong enough to say something before a single word is spoken.

His editorial work has been published in L'Officiel and featured by Vogue Italia, and he has been recognized as a CAW26 Spotlight Talent through AFA × Vogue. On the client side, his styling has shaped seven Ola Reay collections — spanning menswear and womenswear across Spring/Summer, Fall/Winter and Resort seasons — alongside private styling for public figures including a celebrity gospel artist and a corporate managing director.

"Styling empowers individuals to express their unique personality, boost confidence, and make a statement through their fashion choices."
