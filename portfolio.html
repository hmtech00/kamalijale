Independent Project · Fashion Film

Like Mother!!! Like Mirror!!!

A self-directed fashion film exploring reflection and inheritance between generations. Creative Director/Stylist: Kamal Ijale Oredein & Lorenzo Corti. Fashion Director: Giulia Martinelli. Coordinator: Enrico Bellenghi. Art Director: Andrea Sinopoli. Camera Operator: Matthias Di Giorno. DOP: Gioia Noè. Photography (stills): Riccardo Fabris. Make-up & hair: Nicole Calderari. Models: Xena Xupanic, Kateryna Zilnyk, Eva Zakharchenko, Alice Ferrari. Shot on location in Milan, Italy. Looks: Jenny Packham, Genny, shoes by Loriblu.

Womenswear Editorial

Corporate Utopia

An independent womenswear editorial styled by Kamal Ijale Oredein & Aliya Kujur. Photography: Dmitrii Shmatov. Make-up & hair: Nicole Calderari. Model: Sara Volpatti (Models Milano). Shot on location in Milan, Italy. Looks: Schiaparelli, Woolrich, SU Official, Ports 1961 with shoes by Scarosso.

"Femininity as fierce, bold and strong — woman as a symbol of power and authority. The duality of strength and vulnerability in the modern world. Inspired by experiencing my mother's power raising her kids all by herself." — Kamal Ijale

Visit Ola Reay
