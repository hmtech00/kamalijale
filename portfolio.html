The six Runway Show photos above are sourced from kamalijale.com's own portfolio page — full show and backstage documentation of an Ola Reay menswear presentation in Porto, Portugal, at the same industrial venue seen in Kamal's own Instagram post (identifiable by a "Castelo do Queijo" sign in the background — a fort in Porto). The original site files them under an "SS25" heading, but since that label was previously found paired with mismatched photos elsewhere on the site (see below), the season name is left off here rather than asserted.

Also unresolved: the original site also credits Dr. Olori Boye Ajayi, Dr. Adeshola Adewunmi and Biodun Ibrahim as clients — their photos haven't been sourced/confirmed yet, so they're left out here rather than guessed. It also lists an "SS25 Women's Lookbook" collection separate from the runway show above; the four photo files that carried that label on kamalijale.com turned out to be the same menswear campaign as SS24 (same model, suit and backdrop) — relabeled above accordingly. Two other files (formerly captioned "SS26 Menswear" and "Survival Instinct") turned out to show womenswear editorials instead — also relabeled, generically, since which named collection they actually belong to couldn't be confirmed.
