Womenswear Editorial

Corporate Utopia

An independent womenswear editorial styled by Kamal Ijale Oredein & Aliya Kujur. Photography: Dmitrii Shmatov. Make-up & hair: Nicole Calderari. Model: Sara Volpatti (Models Milano). Shot on location in Milan, Italy. Looks: Schiaparelli, Woolrich, SU Official, Ports 1961 with shoes by Scarosso.

"Femininity as fierce, bold and strong — woman as a symbol of power and authority. The duality of strength and vulnerability in the modern world. Inspired by experiencing my mother's power raising her kids all by herself." — Kamal Ijale

Brand Partnership

Ola Reay

Visit Ola Reay

A multi-year creative partnership with the Nigerian fashion brand Ola Reay, co-creating and styling the runway collection shown at Portugal Fashion Week under CANEX, leading creative direction and styling for the SS25 campaign & lookbook, the "Friends" campaign/e-commerce, and the SS25 "DREAM" womenswear e-commerce & lookbook.

"Survival Instincts" (SS26 runway concept): "Despite systemic barriers and the constant struggle to be seen and heard, the average Black man learns to adapt, endure, and persist without abandoning his ambition... Everyone deserves the space to be seen, to be heard, to express fully." — Kamal Ijale
