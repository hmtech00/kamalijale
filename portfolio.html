Editorial Projects

Two editorial productions from Kamal's portfolio: a fashion film for L'Officiel Italia and an independent womenswear editorial, both shot in Milan.

L'Officiel Italia · Fashion Film

L'Officiel Italia

A fashion film for L'Officiel Italia exploring reflection and inheritance between generations, styled by Kamal Ijale Oredein & Lorenzo Corti. Fashion Director: Giulia Martinelli. Photography (stills): Riccardo Fabris. Make-up & hair: Nicole Calderari. Models: Xena Xupanic, Kateryna Zilnyk, Eva Zakharchenko, Alice Ferrari. Shot on location in Milan, Italy. Looks: Jenny Packham, Genny, shoes by Loriblu.
